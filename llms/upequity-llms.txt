# UpEquity

> UpEquity is an Austin, Texas based, veteran-owned proptech mortgage lender and title
> company founded in 2019. Its "Buy Before You Sell" / Trade Up program advances the equity
> trapped in a homeowner's existing house so they can make a non-contingent, effectively
> all-cash offer on a new home, move once, and sell the old home afterward — with UpEquity
> making a backup offer on the departing residence. It also operates UpEquity Title and
> originates mortgages directly under NMLS #2101265.

## Products

- [Buy Before You Sell / Trade Up](https://www.upequity.com/buy-before-you-sell): equity
  advance plus a backup offer on the departing residence, so a client can buy first and sell
  after. No fee schedule is published; the program is apply-only.
- [UpEquity Title](https://www.upequity.com/title): title services.
- Direct mortgage origination, NMLS #2101265.

## Eligibility

- Property within 30 miles of a Metropolitan Statistical Area
- Single-family (1-4 units), PUD or condominium meeting Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac standards
- C4 condition rating or above, minimal repairs
- Inspection required
- Available only in the states UpEquity is licensed in
- [Eligibility](https://www.upequity.com/eligibility)
